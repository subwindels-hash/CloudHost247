Acceptable Use Policy

Official CloudHost247 policy. Versioned and dated; the latest published version governs.

Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-10-05

1. Prohibited use

  • Illegal content or activity, including content that infringes intellectual property rights.
  • Malware distribution, phishing, botnet command-and-control or scanning of networks you do not own.
  • Spam, unsolicited bulk messaging or abusive automated traffic.
  • Attempts to compromise the platform, other customers, or circumvent service limits.

2. Resource use

Services must be used within their published limits. Sustained abuse of shared resources may lead to throttling, suspension or migration to an appropriate service.

3. Enforcement

Violations are handled proportionally: notice and remediation first, suspension where conduct continues or is severe. Illegal content is escalated to the relevant authorities where required.
