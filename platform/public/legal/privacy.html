Privacy Policy

Official CloudHost247 policy. Versioned and dated; the latest published version governs.

Version 1.0 · Effective 2026-10-05

1. Data we process

Account data (name, email, authentication records), billing data (invoices, payment status), service data (domains, server configurations you create) and technical logs needed to operate and secure the platform.

2. Why we process it

  • To provide and operate the services you order.
  • To bill for services and prevent fraud.
  • To secure the platform and investigate abuse.
  • To meet legal obligations.

3. Protection

Credentials are stored using strong password hashing; secrets such as provider tokens and TOTP seeds are encrypted at rest. Access to personal data is role-controlled and audited.

4. Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Data is shared only with processors required to deliver the service (for example, registries for domain registration) and where law requires.

5. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction you may have rights of access, correction, deletion and portability. Contact us via the contact page to exercise them.

6. Retention

We retain data while your account is active and for the period required by law or legitimate operational needs afterwards.
