Move Your Website to CloudHost247

Changing hosts should not be scary. Our migration process is guided, transparent and designed to avoid downtime.

  1. Choose your hosting plan

    Pick the service that fits your website — web hosting, WordPress, cloud or VPS.

  2. Provide your migration details

    Current host, control panel access or backup location — whatever applies to your setup.

  3. CloudHost247 prepares the migration

    We review the source environment and prepare the destination account.

  4. Website is transferred

    Files, databases and mail are copied and verified on the new infrastructure.

  5. DNS is updated

    Nameservers or records are switched over once everything checks out.

  6. Website goes live

    Traffic flows to CloudHost247 and we monitor the first hours closely.

Migration is performed with your existing host still live, so there is no window where your site is nowhere.
