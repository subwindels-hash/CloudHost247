Trademark & Copyright Infringement Policy At CloudHost247 Isc, we respect and are committed to protecting intellectual property rights. This policy outlines how we review and respond to claims of trademark and copyright infringement and how we assist rights holders in protecting their legal interests. Domain Name Dispute Claims This policy applies to disputes involving CloudHost247 Isc products and services, excluding domain name disputes. If you have a concern regarding a registered domain name, you must follow the appropriate dispute resolution procedures:  For general domain disputes, refer to the Uniform Domain Name Dispute Resolution Policy (UDRP). All such claims should be submitted to an ICANN-approved dispute resolution provider.  For.ng domain names, disputes must be directed to the Nigeria Internet Registration Association (NIRA) and resolved in accordance with the NIRA Complaints Policy. Trademark Claims To report a trademark infringement, the complaining party should send an email to: abuse@cloudhost247isc.com with the subject line: "Trademark Claim" Your email must include the following:  Full name, address, email address, and phone number of the trademark owner  A copy of the trademark certificate or official acceptance letter  Date the trademark was first used  Description of how the trademark is being infringed  Full details (name, address, email, phone) of the alleged infringer  Evidence showing that the alleged infringer is a CloudHost247 Isc customer Upon receiving a valid complaint, CloudHost247 Isc will investigate and forward the claim to the accused party. During the investigation, we may temporarily restrict access to or remove the disputed material.

If the claim is valid, we may permanently remove or suspend access to the infringing material or take other appropriate action. If the claim is found invalid, access to the material will be restored. This policy does not override the UDRP or NIRA Complaints Policy, and all parties remain subject to applicable ICANN regulations. Copyright Claims To submit a copyright infringement complaint, please email: abuse@cloudhost247.com with the subject line: "Copyright Claim" Your submission must include:  Identification of the copyrighted work being infringed  Proof of copyright registration (if available)  Description or location of the infringing material hosted by a CloudHost247 Isc customer  Full contact details of the copyright owner (name, address, email, phone)  Details of the alleged infringer (name, address, email, phone)  A statement confirming that the use of the material was not authorized by the copyright owner Upon receipt, CloudHost247 Isc will investigate and notify the alleged infringer. We may temporarily remove or disable access to the disputed content during the review process. If the complaint is valid, we may permanently remove or suspend access to the content. If not, the content will be restored promptly. Counter Notification Policy If you believe content was removed or disabled due to a mistake or misidentification, you may submit a counter-notification by emailing: abuse@cloudhost247.com Your counter-notification must include:  Identification of the material removed or disabled and its previous location

 A statement under penalty of perjury that the material was removed or disabled by mistake or misidentification  Your full name, address, email address, and phone number Once received, CloudHost247 Isc will forward your counter-notification to the original complainant. The complainant will then have 10 business days to seek a court order preventing restoration of the material. If no legal action is taken within that period, the material may be restored within 14 business days. Repeated Infringement Policy CloudHost247 Isc reserves the right to suspend or terminate accounts of users who are repeatedly found to violate intellectual property rights, including copyright and trademark infringement.
