Cybercrime Detection Policy 1. Purpose CloudHost247 Isc ("the Company") is committed to ensuring that all domains registered, hosted, or managed on its platform ("the Platform") are not used for illegal, fraudulent, or malicious activities. This policy explains the preventive and detection measures used by the Company to identify suspicious or fraudulent domains, as well as the procedures for handling complaints related to cybercrime or abuse involving domains on the Platform. It also outlines how individuals or organizations can report and resolve concerns relating to fraud or other cyber-related offenses involving domains hosted by the Company. 2. Scope This policy applies to:  All employees of CloudHost247 Isc  All visitors and users of the Platform  All registered clients (Registrants)  All complainants and reporting parties 3. Fraudulent Domain Definition A fraudulent domain refers to any domain name or hosted service that is used to carry out fraud, deception, or any form of cybercrime. This includes, but is not limited to:  Identity theft or impersonation websites  Cyberterrorism-related activities  Forgery or document falsification services  Advance fee fraud schemes  Online financial scams  Domains designed to imitate banks, fintech platforms, or recognized financial institutions CloudHost247 Isc strictly prohibits the registration or hosting of fraudulent domains. If such domains are detected or reported, they will be immediately suspended or removed following investigation by the Abuse Team. 4. Fraud Prevention and Detection

The CloudHost247 Isc Abuse Team conducts regular monitoring and security reviews of domains hosted on the Platform to identify suspicious or potentially fraudulent activity. When a suspicious domain is identified, the Abuse Team will:  Contact the Registrant for clarification regarding the domain's purpose and associated business activity  Request supporting documentation or verification where necessary If the Registrant provides valid and verifiable information confirming legitimate use, the domain will remain active. If the Registrant fails to respond within 7 working days, or cannot prove legitimate use of the domain, the Company reserves the right to:  Suspend the domain immediately  Terminate hosting services associated with the domain 5. Complaints and Reporting If any individual suspects that a domain or service hosted on CloudHost247 Isc is being used for fraudulent or criminal purposes, they should report it via email to: support@cloudhost247.com Upon receipt of a complaint, the Abuse Team will:  Review and assess the report  Notify the affected Registrant when necessary  Suspend the domain or service if evidence of abuse is found Registrant contact details will only be disclosed:  When legally required through a valid court order or law enforcement request CloudHost247 Isc may also, at its discretion, escalate serious cases to relevant law enforcement authorities for further investigation. 6. Contact Information To report abuse, fraud, or suspicious activity involving any domain hosted on CloudHost247 Isc, please contact: Email: support@cloudhost247.com
