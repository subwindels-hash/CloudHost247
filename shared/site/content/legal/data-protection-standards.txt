CloudHost247 Isc: Data Protection Standards Effective Date: August 14, 2025 At CloudHost247 Isc, safeguarding the personal data of our customers, partners, and employees is a top priority. These Data Protection Standards outline the principles, measures, and responsibilities we follow to ensure compliance with applicable data protection laws, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), and other relevant regulations. 1. Purpose The purpose of this policy is to:  Protect personal data from unauthorized access, loss, alteration, or disclosure.  Ensure compliance with all applicable privacy and data protection regulations.  Maintain the trust of our customers, employees, and partners. 2. Scope These standards apply to:  All employees, contractors, and partners of CloudHost247 Isc.  All systems, processes, and technologies used to store, process, or transmit personal data.  All personal data collected through our websites, services, support channels, and business operations. 3. Data Protection Principles We follow these core principles when handling personal data: 1. Lawfulness, Fairness, and Transparency Data is processed lawfully and transparently. 2. Purpose Limitation Data is collected for specific, legitimate purposes and not processed beyond those purposes. 3. Data Minimization Only the minimum necessary personal data is collected and retained. 4. Accuracy Personal data is kept accurate and up-to-date.

5. Storage Limitation Data is kept only as long as necessary for business or legal purposes. 6. Integrity and Confidentiality Appropriate security measures are implemented to protect data. 7. Accountability We maintain records and proof of compliance. 4. Data Collection and Use  We collect personal data only for purposes related to the provision of our services, customer support, and legal compliance.  Data may include contact details, billing information, technical usage data, and support communications.  Sensitive data is collected only when strictly necessary and with explicit consent. 5. Data Security Measures To protect data, CloudHost247 Isc implements:  Encryption of data in transit (TLS/SSL) and at rest.  Access controls to limit data access to authorized personnel only.  Multi-factor authentication (MFA) for administrative accounts.  Regular security audits and penetration testing.  Firewalls and intrusion detection/prevention systems (IDS/IPS).  Daily backups stored in secure, encrypted locations. 6. Data Retention  Personal data is retained only for as long as necessary for its intended purpose, in line with our Privacy Policy and legal requirements.  After retention periods expire, data is securely deleted or anonymized. 7. Data Sharing  We only share personal data with trusted third-party service providers, and only when necessary to deliver our services.  All third-party providers must comply with strict contractual data protection obligations.  We do not sell personal data to third parties.

8. Rights of Data Subjects Under applicable laws, individuals have the right to:  Access their personal data.  Request correction or deletion.  Restrict or object to processing.  Request data portability.  Withdraw consent at any time (where applicable). Requests can be made by contacting privacy@cloudhost247.com. 9. Incident Response  All data breaches or suspected breaches are reported immediately to our Data Protection Officer (DPO).  We investigate and respond promptly; including notifying affected individuals and authorities when required by law. 10. Employee Responsibilities  All employees handling personal data must follow these standards.  Employees receive regular data protection and cyber security training.  Access to personal data is restricted to those who need it to perform their duties. 11. Compliance and Monitoring We review and update these standards regularly to ensure compliance with evolving regulations and industry best practices. Internal audits are conducted at least annually.
