Data Privacy Notice and Consent Form 1. Introduction CloudHost247 Isc ("we", "our", or "the Company") is committed to protecting the privacy and personal data of all prospective employees in compliance with the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023 and other applicable data protection regulations. This Privacy Notice explains how we collect, use, store, share, and protect your personal data when you apply for employment through our Careers page or any of our recruitment channels. By submitting your application, you confirm that you have read and understood this Notice and consent to the processing of your personal data as described herein. 2. Personal Data We Collect During the recruitment process, we may collect and process the following categories of personal data: a. Identity Information  Full name  Date of birth  Gender (where voluntarily provided)  Photograph (if included in CV) b. Contact Information  Email address  Phone number  Residential address c. Professional & Employment Information  Curriculum Vitae (CV)  Cover letter  Employment history  Educational background  Certifications and qualifications  References  LinkedIn or professional profile links  Guarantor's details (name, organization, position, contact information)

 Employment verification and reference feedback from previous employers d. Sensitive Personal Data (if voluntarily provided)  Health information  Disability status (for accommodation purposes)  Background check information (where applicable and legally permitted) We request that you only provide information relevant to your job application. Where you provide personal data of a guarantor or referee, you confirm that you have informed the individual and obtained their consent for their data to be shared with us for recruitment purposes. 3. Purpose of Processing We process your personal data for the following lawful purposes:  To assess your suitability for employment  To communicate with you regarding your application  To conduct interviews and recruitment evaluations  To perform background, employment verification, or reference checks (where applicable)  To maintain recruitment records  To comply with legal and regulatory obligations We do not use your personal data for unrelated purposes without obtaining additional consent where required. 4. Lawful Basis for Processing In accordance with the NDPA 2023, we rely on the following lawful bases:  Consent - where you voluntarily submit your application  Pre-contractual necessity - processing required before entering into an employment contract  Legal obligation - where required by Nigerian law  Legitimate interest - for recruitment and talent management, provided such interests do not override your fundamental rights

5. Data Sharing and Disclosure We may share your personal data with:  Internal HR and hiring managers  Background screening providers (where applicable)  IT service providers supporting recruitment systems  Regulatory or law enforcement authorities (where legally required) We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties. Where third parties process data on our behalf, we ensure they are bound by confidentiality and data protection obligations in line with the NDPA. 6. Data Retention If your application is unsuccessful, we may retain your data for 6 - 12 months for future opportunities, unless you request earlier deletion. If your application is successful, your data will form part of your employee record and be retained in accordance with our internal employee data policies. After the retention period, your data will be securely deleted or anonymized. 7. International Data Transfers As a technology and hosting service provider, some of our systems may be hosted outside Nigeria. Where your personal data is transferred internationally, we ensure that:  Adequate safeguards are in place  Transfers comply with NDPA cross-border data protection requirements 8. Data Security We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures, including:

 Secure servers and encrypted connections (SSL)  Access controls and authentication systems  Confidentiality obligations for staff  Regular monitoring and system security reviews 9. Your Rights Under the NDPA Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act, you have the right to:  Access your personal data  Request correction of inaccurate data  Request deletion (right to erasure), subject to legal limits  Withdraw consent at any time  Object to processing in certain circumstances  Request restriction of processing  Lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) To exercise your rights, contact: dpo@cloudhost247.com 10. Withdrawal of Consent You may withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before consent was withdrawn. However, withdrawing consent during an active recruitment process may limit our ability to continue considering your application. 11. Consent Declaration By submitting your application through our Careers page or via email, you confirm that:  The information provided is accurate and truthful  You consent to the collection and processing of your personal data for recruitment purposes as described above  You understand your rights under the Nigeria Data Protection Act (NDPA) 2023
