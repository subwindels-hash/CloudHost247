Domain Name Auto-Renewal and Deletion Policy Domain Name Auto-Renewal CloudHost247 Isc provides an optional Auto-Renew feature to ensure uninterrupted domain service for all registrants. When enabled, your domain will be automatically renewed using available funds in your account balance or via the credit/debit card you have securely saved in our system. If sufficient funds are not available at the time of renewal, we will notify you via email so you can complete the renewal manually. To help prevent service interruption or domain loss, domain registrations may be set to Auto Renew by default. By using this feature, you authorize CloudHost247 Isc to securely store and charge your selected payment method during domain registration, renewal, or transfer processes.  Auto-renewals are processed for a 1-year extension per cycle.  Renewal charges will be based on the current applicable rates at the time of renewal, which may differ from the original purchase price.  You may disable Auto-Renewal at any time from your account settings. Domain Name Expiration and Deletion If payment is not successfully processed on or before the domain renewal date, your domain will expire.  Within 1 day after expiration: The domain may be deactivated and replaced with a parking page indicating expiration. Associated services such as websites or email may stop functioning.  From approximately 36 days after expiration: The domain may become available for registration by third parties. If another party acquires the domain, it can no longer be recovered or renewed by you.  Redemption Period (around 40 days after expiration): If the domain is not purchased by a third party, it may enter the registry redemption period as defined by the domain registry. During this stage, recovery may still be possible, but a redemption fee plus renewal charges will apply. Fees vary depending on the domain extension (TLD).  After redemption period: The domain may be held briefly (typically up to five days) by the registry before being released for general public registration.
