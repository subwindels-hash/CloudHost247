Backup Policy 1. Overview Backups play a critical role in the management and security of websites, applications, and email services. By storing copies of your website files, databases, configurations, and emails in a secure location, you can restore your data in the event of system failure, cyber incidents, accidental deletion, or service disruption. Backup services are not automatically included in all CloudHost247 Isc plans. They may be offered as optional paid features for selected services. It remains your sole responsibility as the customer to maintain regular backups of your data and store them in a secure location separate from your hosting account. Using your hosting account as a backup or storage repository is not permitted. We strongly recommend the use of trusted third-party offsite backup solutions such as CodeGuard, JetBackup, or similar services to ensure additional data protection. Although CloudHost247 Isc may perform periodic backups for certain services, we do not guarantee their availability, completeness, or restoration success. We are not liable for any data loss under any circumstances. Our control panel may provide tools that allow you to manually create, download, and restore backups where applicable. Please note that CloudHost247 Isc does not maintain or control third-party backup systems used by infrastructure partners on a per-server or per-package basis. 2. Shared Hosting CloudHost247 Isc performs automated weekly backups for shared hosting environments. This includes, but is not limited to:  Web Hosting  WordPress Hosting  Node.js Hosting  Python Hosting  Reseller Hosting  Any other shared hosting services provided

Users may also create, download, and restore backups via the hosting control panel, especially when applications are installed using tools like Softaculous. Backup limitations may apply:  A maximum of 5 Softaculous backups OR  Up to 15% of total hosting storage capacity Excessive or recursive backups ("backups of backups") are not allowed. Customers may request restoration or access to available backups while their hosting account remains active. If the account is suspended due to non-payment, renewal is required before any backup access is granted. For terminated accounts, backups may no longer be available as they are periodically overwritten. If a backup is available, a restoration fee may apply. Restoration requests can be submitted to: billing@cloudhost247isc.com Note: Backup restoration does not replace account renewal fees where applicable. We strongly recommend maintaining independent offsite backups using trusted third-party services. 3. Enterprise Servers Backup services for enterprise infrastructure are optional and may require an additional subscription at the time of purchase or deployment. Once enabled, server backups are typically:  Performed weekly at system level  Stored within the same or designated secure data center  Retained for up to four (4) weeks Backups older than 30 days are automatically overwritten. Customers are responsible for maintaining external copies of critical or historical data before retention cycles overwrite existing backups. CloudHost247 Isc does not guarantee recovery of any specific state of a server at any time. For Dedicated and Virtual Servers, backup solutions can be configured upon request, including additional storage or secondary backup servers.

Restoration Policy  Full server restoration is available from any of the last four weekly backups (if backup service is active)  Partial restoration is not supported, as backups are system-level snapshots  No backups are created for servers without an active backup subscription Termination Policy Once an enterprise server is terminated, all associated backups are permanently deleted and cannot be recovered. Customers are responsible for exporting data before termination. Enterprise backup requests: enterprise@cloudhost247isc.com 4. Professional Email & Workspace Email backup availability depends on server retention policies and how email clients are configured. If you use third-party email applications (e.g., Outlook, Thunderbird) configured to delete messages from the server after download, those messages will only exist locally on your device and cannot be restored from CloudHost247 Isc. Backups for Professional Email & Workspace services are maintained at the infrastructure level, and CloudHost247 Isc does not directly control or guarantee their retention beyond system policies. For suspended accounts, backup access requires account renewal. Accounts remain in a suspended state for 20 days before permanent termination, after which all data and backups are permanently deleted. 5.
