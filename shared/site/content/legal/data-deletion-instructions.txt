CloudHost247 Isc: Data Deletion Instructions Effective Date: August 14, 2025 At CloudHost247 Isc, we respect your right to control your personal data. This document provides step-by-step instructions for requesting the deletion of your personal information in compliance with applicable data protection laws, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). 1. Eligibility for Data Deletion You may request deletion of your personal data if:  You no longer use our services.  You withdraw your consent for processing (where applicable).  The data is no longer necessary for the purpose it was collected.  You believe your data has been unlawfully processed. Certain legal obligations (such as tax, fraud prevention, or dispute resolution requirements) may require us to retain some information temporarily even after a deletion request. 2. What Data Can Be Deleted We can delete the following:  Account profile details (name, email, contact number)  Billing and payment history (after legal retention periods)  Service usage logs and communications  Stored files, emails, and hosting data associated with your account We cannot delete data that we are legally required to keep for regulatory or compliance purposes until the retention period expire. 3. How to Request Data Deletion Follow these steps to request deletion:

Step 1 Submit Your Request Send an email to privacy@cloudhost247.com with the subject line: Data Deletion Request. Include:  Full name  Account username or customer ID  Registered email address  A brief statement confirming you wish to have your data deleted Step 2 Identity Verification we will verify your identity to protect your account from unauthorized requests. This may involve:  Sending a confirmation code to your registered email address  Requesting a copy of a valid ID (for high-risk requests) Step 3 Processing Your Request once verified:  We will delete your data within 30 calendar days (or sooner if possible).  You will receive a confirmation email when deletion is complete. 4. Impact of Data Deletion After your data is deleted:  You will lose access to your account and all services linked to it.  All backups, files, and hosted content will be permanently erased.  The action is irreversible once deleted; we cannot recover your data. 5. Exceptions We may retain certain information if required to:  Comply with legal obligations  Resolve disputes  Enforce agreements  Maintain necessary business records for accounting, tax, or security purposes

6. Contact Information For any questions or concerns regarding data deletion, contact: privacy@cloudhost247.com www.cloudhost247.com
